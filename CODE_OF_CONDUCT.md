# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers
pledge to make participation in the Age of Legends project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its players

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
