# Game Vision

## Purpose

Age of Legends is a modern, persistent and highly scalable MMORPG. It combines robust,
authoritative server engineering with a deep, socially driven gameplay loop centred on
exploration, kingdom building and long-term progression.

The goal is **not** to reproduce any existing game. The goal is to create an original intellectual
property — with its own universe, characters, visual identity, story, mechanics and art direction.
External references inform engineering discipline and design principles only; no protected content
is reproduced.

## Mission

Build a commercial MMORPG capable of operating continuously for many years, offering a deep,
social, scalable and enjoyable experience to a large concurrent population of players.

## Vision Statement

Age of Legends unites, in a single original world:

- real-time exploration of a continuous map;
- construction and evolution of a persistent kingdom;
- durable, meaningful progression;
- a shared online world with a living economy;
- cooperative PvE and structured PvP;
- guilds, global events and world bosses;
- intelligent NPCs and a modern, modular architecture.

## What Success Looks Like

Success is measured by the quality of the architecture, the depth of the gameplay, the ease of
extension, technical stability, the player experience and the originality of the intellectual
property. Every decision converges on those measures.

## Two Guiding Questions

When a hard problem appears during development, the team answers two questions before designing a
third, original solution:

1. **Engineering** — what is the most robust way to build this, drawing on proven MMORPG server
   architecture?
2. **Gameplay** — what makes this most satisfying to play, drawing on proven progression and
   retention design?

The resulting implementation keeps the technical robustness of the first answer, the play quality
of the second, and remains entirely original.
