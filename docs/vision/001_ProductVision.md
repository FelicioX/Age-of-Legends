# Product Vision

## Elevator Pitch

Age of Legends is a browser- and mobile-first MMORPG where players grow a single outpost into a
legendary kingdom, recruit a roster of heroes, and lead them across a persistent, shared continent
of resources to gather, dungeons to clear, world bosses to fell and rival guilds to outmanoeuvre.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** players who enjoy long-horizon progression and base-building games and want a
  deeper, more social and more skill-expressive experience.
- **Secondary:** cooperative and competitive MMORPG players looking for an accessible,
  cross-platform world that respects their time.
- **Tertiary:** strategy and management players drawn to economy, optimization and guild
  coordination.

## Platforms

A single codebase targets modern web browsers and mobile devices. The game is designed for both
short mobile sessions and longer sessions on larger screens, with a responsive interface and
input-agnostic controls.

## Player Promise

- **Your progress is permanent.** The world and your kingdom persist and evolve.
- **Fairness first.** Monetization never sells power; skill, planning and time are what advance you.
- **You are never alone.** The world is shared, cooperative and competitive by design.
- **Respect for your time.** Meaningful sessions at any length, with automation for the busywork.

## Business Framing

Age of Legends is built as a long-lived, live-service product. The architecture, tooling and
LiveOps design assume years of continuous content and a large, growing population. Ethical
monetization (cosmetics, convenience and season passes) sustains development without compromising
game balance.

## Differentiators

- A single deterministic simulation core shared by server, client prediction and tests.
- Genuinely intelligent NPCs and a reactive, event-driven world.
- A dynamic, carefully tuned economy with real sinks and faucets.
- A modular, extensible platform designed for rapid, safe content addition.
