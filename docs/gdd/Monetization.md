# Monetization

## Principle

Monetization is ethical and strictly non-pay-to-win. Purchases save time or express identity; they
never sell power or competitive advantage. This is a hard design constraint, not a guideline.

## What Is Sold

- **Cosmetics** — appearances, skins, effects, titles, kingdom decorations. No stat impact.
- **Convenience** — additional queues, storage, quality-of-life features that do not alter combat
  or economy balance.
- **Season pass** — a cosmetic/progression track earned by playing; the paid tier adds cosmetic
  rewards, not power.
- **Premium currency** — used only for the above categories.

## What Is Never Sold

- Combat power, exclusive winning heroes/equipment, or stat advantages.
- Randomized paid boxes that grant power ("pay-to-win loot boxes").
- Anything that makes spending strictly required to remain competitive.

## Fairness Safeguards

- All power is obtainable through play.
- PvP and competitive modes are balanced independent of spending (see [PvP.md](PvP.md)).
- Convenience purchases are capped so they cannot compound into a power gap.

## Economy Interaction

Premium currency does not bypass the in-game economy's sinks in ways that cause inflation or
devalue player effort. Interactions are reviewed against the economy model (see
[Economy.md](Economy.md)).

## Transparency

Odds and contents (where any randomized cosmetic exists) are disclosed. Pricing and value are clear.
Players always know what they are buying.
