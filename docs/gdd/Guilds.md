# Guilds

## Concept

Guilds are persistent player organizations that anchor the social experience. They provide shared
progression, cooperative content, coordination tools and a home for competition.

## Membership & Roles

- **Leader** — full administration, disbands, promotes/demotes.
- **Officers** — manage members, events and shared resources.
- **Members** — participate, contribute and earn.

Roles carry granular permissions to keep large guilds manageable.

## Guild Progression

- **Guild level** — advanced by member activity and contributions; unlocks perks and capacity.
- **Guild technologies/perks** — shared bonuses purchased with guild contributions.
- **Guild hall** — a shared space enabling guild-only features and buffs.

## Cooperative Content

- **Guild events** — scheduled cooperative objectives with shared rewards.
- **World bosses** — large encounters that reward coordination (see [Bosses.md](Bosses.md)).
- **Guild quests** — collective goals that progress the guild and its members.

## Coordination Tools

- Guild chat and announcements.
- Event scheduling and rally points.
- Shared storage/contribution systems with clear auditing.

## Competition

- **Territory control** — guilds contest strategic points on the map for benefits.
- **Guild rankings** — leaderboards for activity, power and event performance.
- **Guild PvP** — structured inter-guild competition (see [PvP.md](PvP.md)).

## Design Intent

Guilds create interdependence and belonging without mandating a heavy time commitment: casual
members contribute meaningfully, while dedicated cores drive competitive play.
