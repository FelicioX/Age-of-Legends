# PvP

## Philosophy

PvP is structured, opt-in where it matters, and skill- and composition-driven. It rewards mastery
and coordination, never simply the largest wallet. Matchmaking and brackets keep competition fair.

## Modes

- **Arena (ranked)** — small-scale party battles with seasonal ranking and matchmaking.
- **Skirmish (casual)** — unranked practice with the same rules, no rating pressure.
- **Raids** — attacking rival kingdoms for contested resources, with attacker/defender rules and
  protection windows to prevent harassment.
- **Territory control** — guild-scale contest of strategic map points (see [Guilds.md](Guilds.md)).

## Fairness Rules

- **Matchmaking** by power and rating to avoid lopsided matches.
- **Protection windows** — new and recently raided players get shields.
- **Loss limits** — a protected portion of resources is never lost (see [Resources.md](Resources.md)).
- **No pay-to-win** — purchases never grant combat advantage (see [Monetization.md](Monetization.md)).

## Rewards & Ranking

- Seasonal ranked ladders with cosmetic and prestige rewards.
- PvP currencies exchangeable for cosmetics and non-power items.
- Leaderboards for players and guilds.

## Anti-Abuse

- Rate limits and cooldowns on raids.
- Detection of collusion and win-trading.
- Server authority prevents client-side manipulation (see
  [../architecture/Security.md](../architecture/Security.md)).
