# Crafting

## Concept

Crafting turns resources and refined materials into equipment, consumables and upgrade components
at the workshop. It is a primary resource sink and a key source of hero power and economic activity.

## Professions

Gathering and crafting professions provide mastery progression:

- **Gathering:** logging, mining, quarrying, foraging — improve yield and speed for their resource.
- **Refining:** carpentry, smithing, masonry — convert raw resources into refined materials.
- **Crafting:** weaponsmithing, armorsmithing, artificing, alchemy — produce equipment and
  consumables.

Professions level with use, unlocking better recipes and efficiencies. See
[Resources.md](Resources.md).

## Recipes

- Recipes define inputs (resources/materials), a workshop tier requirement, a craft time and an
  output with a quality range.
- Higher-tier recipes require refined materials and higher profession levels.
- Recipes are data-driven to support tooling and rapid content addition.

## Quality & Affixes

Crafted equipment rolls a quality tier and affixes (attribute bonuses, set identifiers). Quality
odds improve with profession mastery and better materials, giving mastery tangible value and
supporting a crafting market.

## Equipment

Equipment fills hero slots (weapon, armor, accessories), contributing attributes and set bonuses.
Items can be upgraded and, at higher tiers, reforged for better affixes — additional sinks.

## Economy Role

Crafting drives demand for gathered/refined materials and supplies the market with equipment,
linking professions, resources and the player economy. See [Economy.md](Economy.md).
