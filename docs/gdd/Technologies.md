# Technologies

## Concept

Research provides persistent, account-wide progression through technology trees unlocked and
advanced at the laboratory. Technologies grant durable bonuses and unlock new capabilities.

## Trees

- **Economy** — production rates, storage, gathering speed, market efficiency.
- **Military** — hero attributes, party capacity, combat modifiers.
- **Kingdom** — construction speed, upgrade discounts, defense.
- **Exploration** — movement, vision, event access, gathering yield.

## Research Model

- Each technology has levels; each level costs resources and time and may require prerequisites and
  a minimum laboratory tier.
- Research runs on timers (queueable; optionally accelerated with non-power convenience items).
- Effects are persistent and stack additively/multiplicatively per defined rules.

## Design Intent

- Technologies provide steady, long-horizon progression that complements hero and kingdom growth.
- Trees present meaningful choices in ordering and specialization rather than a single optimal path.
- Bonuses are bounded to avoid runaway power creep; they broaden options more than they inflate
  numbers.
