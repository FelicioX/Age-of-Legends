# Gameplay

## Overview

Gameplay in Age of Legends spans two interlocking layers:

1. **Kingdom layer** — a persistent, top-down base the player builds, upgrades and defends. This is
   the home of resource production, research, hero management and progression.
2. **World layer** — a shared, continuous continent the player explores with hero parties to
   gather, fight, complete quests, join events and confront other players.

Players move fluidly between the two: the world funds the kingdom; the kingdom empowers the world.

## Player Verbs

Explore · Gather · Build · Upgrade · Recruit · Train · Equip · Craft · Fight · Cooperate · Compete ·
Trade · Research · Conquer · Expand.

## Kingdom Layer

- Place and upgrade buildings on the kingdom grid.
- Manage the five resources and their production chains.
- Recruit heroes, assign them to parties, and develop their attributes, gear and skills.
- Queue research in laboratories to unlock persistent bonuses.
- Defend against raids and dispatch parties on world activities.

## World Layer

- Travel a continuous map revealed through fog-of-war exploration.
- Send parties to resource nodes to gather over time or actively.
- Enter dungeons and encounters for loot and progression.
- Encounter dynamic events, world bosses and rival players.
- Claim territory and contest strategic points with a guild.

## Controls & Input

Input-agnostic by design: touch, mouse and keyboard are equally supported. Core interactions
(select, move, target, use skill, confirm) map cleanly to a tap/click and a small set of gestures
or hotkeys.

## Difficulty & Onboarding

- A guided early game teaches one system at a time (build → gather → recruit → fight).
- Difficulty scales with kingdom and hero power; content is gated so players are prepared.
- Optional challenge tiers reward mastery without gating core progression.
