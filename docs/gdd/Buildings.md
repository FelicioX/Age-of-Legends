# Buildings

## Concept

The kingdom is a grid of buildings the player places, upgrades and arranges. Buildings produce
resources, unlock features, train heroes and enable research. The town hall gates overall kingdom
progression.

## Core Buildings

| Building     | Function                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Town Hall    | Sets kingdom level; gates the tier of all other buildings/features  |
| Farm         | Produces food                                                       |
| Mine         | Produces iron                                                       |
| Quarry       | Produces stone                                                      |
| Sawmill      | Produces wood                                                       |
| Warehouse    | Increases resource storage capacity and raid protection             |
| Barracks     | Recruits and trains troops/heroes; raises party capacity            |
| Laboratory   | Runs research (see [Technologies.md](Technologies.md))              |
| Workshop     | Crafts equipment and items (see [Crafting.md](Crafting.md))         |
| Market       | Enables trade and the player market (see [Economy.md](Economy.md))  |

Additional buildings are introduced through progression and events.

## Upgrade Model

- Each building has tiers. Upgrading consumes resources and time and often requires a minimum town
  hall level.
- Upgrades increase output, capacity or unlock new options.
- Construction and upgrades run on timers; players may queue and, optionally, accelerate them with
  non-power convenience items.

## Layout & Adjacency

Placement matters: certain buildings grant adjacency bonuses, encouraging thoughtful kingdom
planning without punishing experimentation (rearranging is allowed).

## Production & Offline

Production accrues over time, including while offline, up to storage capacity. This makes the
kingdom idle-friendly while rewarding active collection and expansion.

## Defense

Warehouses protect a portion of stored resources from raids; defensive structures and troop
garrisons mitigate PvP raids (see [PvP.md](PvP.md)).
