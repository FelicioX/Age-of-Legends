# Game Design Document — Age of Legends

**Status:** Living document · **Owner:** Game Design · **Last updated:** 2026-07-24

This is the master Game Design Document. It defines the game at a high level and links to detailed
per-system documents. Where this document and a system document disagree, the system document is
authoritative for that system and this document is updated to match.

## 1. Overview

Age of Legends is a persistent, cross-platform MMORPG built around exploration, kingdom building
and long-term progression in a shared, living world. Players develop a kingdom, recruit and grow a
roster of heroes, gather and refine resources, and engage in cooperative PvE, structured PvP,
dynamic world events and guild play.

- **Genre:** MMORPG (exploration, base building, persistent progression)
- **Perspective:** top-down 2D
- **Platforms:** web browser and mobile
- **Session design:** meaningful at any length, from a few minutes to hours

## 2. Objectives

The player's overarching objectives:

- grow a kingdom from a single outpost to a legendary power;
- explore and reveal a continuous continent;
- build a versatile, powerful hero roster;
- master combat, crafting and the economy;
- rise within a guild and shape the shared world.

## 3. Target Audience

See [Product Vision](../vision/001_ProductVision.md). In brief: progression- and base-building
fans seeking a deeper, more social, cross-platform experience.

## 4. Core Loop

The primary loop, repeated at every scale of play:

```
Explore ─▶ Gather ─▶ Build & Upgrade ─▶ Recruit & Train Heroes ─▶
Fight (PvE/PvP/Events) ─▶ Earn rewards & progression ─▶ Unlock new frontiers ─▶ (repeat)
```

Detailed pacing and secondary loops: [GameLoop.md](GameLoop.md).

## 5. Secondary Loops

- **Economy loop:** gather → craft → trade → reinvest.
- **Social loop:** join a guild → coordinate → contribute → shared rewards.
- **Mastery loop:** research → specialize → optimize hero and kingdom builds.
- **LiveOps loop:** seasonal events → limited goals → exclusive cosmetics and titles.

## 6. Progression

Progression is durable and multi-track so players always have a meaningful next step:

- **Kingdom level** (gated by the town hall) unlocks buildings, capacities and features.
- **Hero progression** — levels, star ranks, equipment and skill upgrades.
- **Research/technology** — persistent account-wide bonuses and unlocks.
- **Professions** — gathering and crafting mastery.
- **Account/season** — cosmetic and prestige tracks.

Detail: [../vision/004_ProjectGoals.md](../vision/004_ProjectGoals.md), and the system docs below.

## 8. World & Setting

A continent recovering from a long age of upheaval, dotted with contested ruins, wild frontiers and
resource-rich regions. Factions vie for influence; the wilderness is home to creatures and
warbands. The setting, lore, names and art are original to Age of Legends. Detail:
[World.md](World.md).

## 9. Heroes & Factions

Players command a roster of heroes drawn from distinct archetypes (e.g. vanguard, ranger, mystic,
warden, artificer), each with attributes, equipment slots and an evolving skill kit. Factions
provide thematic identity, synergies and progression flavor. Detail: [Heroes.md](Heroes.md).

## 10. Combat

Real-time, party-based combat with skills, cooldowns, buffs/debuffs, threat and tactical enemy AI.
Encounters scale from solo PvE to guild-scale world bosses. Detail: [Combat.md](Combat.md).

## 11. Building & Resources

The kingdom is built and upgraded from the town hall outward — farms, mines, sawmills, barracks and
laboratories — consuming and producing the five core resources (wood, stone, iron, food, gold).
Detail: [Buildings.md](Buildings.md), [Resources.md](Resources.md).

## 12. Economy & Crafting

Professions, crafting, a player market and direct trade form a dynamic economy with deliberate
sinks and faucets. Detail: [Economy.md](Economy.md), [Crafting.md](Crafting.md).

## 13. Multiplayer, Guilds & Social

A shared, authoritative world with chat, groups and guilds. Guilds unlock cooperative content,
shared progression and structured competition. Detail: [Guilds.md](Guilds.md).

## 14. PvE, PvP & World Bosses

Cooperative dungeons and events (PvE), structured competitive modes (PvP) and large-scale world
bosses that call for coordination. Detail: [PvE.md](PvE.md), [PvP.md](PvP.md), [Bosses.md](Bosses.md).

## 15. Events & LiveOps

A dynamic event system drives the living world; LiveOps layers seasonal content, limited-time goals
and cosmetic rewards on top. Detail: [Events.md](Events.md), [LiveOps.md](LiveOps.md).

## 16. Monetization

Ethical and non-pay-to-win: cosmetics, convenience and season passes. Detail:
[Monetization.md](Monetization.md).

## 17. Risks

Key risks and mitigations are tracked in [../roadmap/Backlog.md](../roadmap/Backlog.md) and the
[risk analysis](../architecture/Scalability.md). Principal risks: economy balance, server
scalability under peak load, content throughput, and retention beyond the early game.

## 18. Open Questions

Open design questions are tracked as issues using the gameplay and balancing templates and resolved
via design review before implementation.
