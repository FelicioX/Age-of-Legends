# PvE

## Concept

Player-versus-environment content is the backbone of progression: dungeons, patrols, quests and
dynamic encounters that reward exploration and party mastery.

## Content Types

- **Dungeons** — instanced, multi-stage encounters with escalating difficulty and curated loot.
- **World encounters** — roaming enemies, camps and ambushes on the open map.
- **Patrols/expeditions** — time-based party dispatches that return resources and loot.
- **Elite challenges** — high-difficulty tiers for skilled players and better rewards.

## Difficulty & Scaling

- Content is tiered by region and kingdom power; players are gated toward content they are prepared
  for.
- Optional higher difficulties raise rewards without blocking core progression.
- Enemy composition and AI escalate with tier (see [../architecture/AI.md](../architecture/AI.md)).

## Rewards

- Resources, equipment, crafting materials, hero progression items and currency.
- Loot tables are tuned to feed progression without trivializing crafting or the market.

## Cooperative PvE

Parties from multiple players can group for tougher dungeons and world bosses (see
[Bosses.md](Bosses.md)), rewarding coordination and role coverage.
