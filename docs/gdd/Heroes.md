# Heroes

## Concept

Heroes are the player's roster of controllable champions. Players collect, develop and combine them
into parties. Heroes are original characters with distinct archetypes, kits and progression.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role                | Fantasy                                       |
| --------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Vanguard  | Frontline / tank    | Absorbs damage, controls space, holds threat  |
| Ranger    | Ranged physical DPS | Sustained precision damage from range         |
| Mystic    | Ranged magical DPS  | Burst and area magical damage                 |
| Warden    | Support / healer    | Healing, shields and cleanses                 |
| Artificer | Control / utility   | Traps, summons, buffs and battlefield control |

Archetypes define role, base stat curves and skill themes. Individual heroes vary within an
archetype.

## Attributes

- **Health** — effective hit points.
- **Attack** — physical/magical power scaling with the hero's damage type.
- **Defense** — mitigation against incoming damage.
- **Speed** — action cadence and turn/initiative pacing.
- **Critical** — chance and multiplier for critical hits.

Attributes derive from base curves, level, star rank, equipment and active buffs.

## Progression

- **Level** — raised through use and training; scales base attributes.
- **Star rank** — milestone tiers that raise ceilings and unlock skill upgrades; advanced via
  duplicate shards or ascension materials.
- **Skills** — an evolving kit (basic, active, passive, ultimate); skills level up to improve
  effect and reduce cooldowns.
- **Equipment** — slots for weapon, armor, and accessories (see [Crafting.md](Crafting.md)).

## Party Composition

Parties are built from a small number of active heroes plus support/formation slots. Composition
matters: role coverage, damage types, and synergy bonuses (archetype and faction) reward
thoughtful team building without a single dominant line-up.

## Factions

Factions provide thematic identity and synergy bonuses when multiple heroes share a faction. They
influence flavor, certain event eligibility and set-style bonuses — never raw power creep that
invalidates other choices.

## Acquisition & Fairness

Heroes are earned through play (quests, events, progression) and, optionally, obtained faster via
non-power monetization. No hero is exclusively purchasable in a way that grants unbeatable power;
see [Monetization.md](Monetization.md).
