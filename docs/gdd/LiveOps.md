# LiveOps

## Concept

LiveOps keeps Age of Legends fresh for years through a steady cadence of seasonal content,
limited-time events, goals and cosmetic rewards — all data-driven and telemetry-informed.

## Pillars

- **Cadence** — regular, predictable content beats (daily, weekly, seasonal).
- **Freshness** — rotating events, featured content and limited goals.
- **Fairness** — LiveOps rewards are cosmetic/convenience; they never sell power (see
  [Monetization.md](Monetization.md)).
- **Data-driven** — telemetry informs balance, pacing and content decisions.

## Content Types

- **Seasons** — themed multi-week arcs with progression tracks and exclusive cosmetics.
- **Limited-time events** — short, focused events with unique goals and rewards (see
  [Events.md](Events.md)).
- **Rotations** — featured dungeons, bosses and challenges.
- **Login and milestone rewards** — retention incentives that respect player time.

## Telemetry & Balance

- Instrument core loops, economy flows and combat outcomes.
- Feed data into balancing (see [Combat.md](Combat.md), [Economy.md](Economy.md)).
- Use A/B-style evaluation for tuning where appropriate.

## Tooling

Events, seasons and rewards are authored as data through dedicated tools (see the `tools/` and
`editor/` areas), enabling rapid, low-risk content creation without engineering changes.
