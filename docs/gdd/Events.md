# Events

## Concept

Events make the world feel alive and reactive. They are time- or trigger-based occurrences on the
map and across the game that create fresh goals, gather players and drive the economy.

## Categories

- **World events** — invasions, resource surges, roaming elites and environmental happenings tied
  to regions, weather and the day/night cycle (see [World.md](World.md)).
- **World bosses** — scheduled/triggered large encounters (see [Bosses.md](Bosses.md)).
- **Seasonal events** — LiveOps content with themed goals and exclusive cosmetics (see
  [LiveOps.md](LiveOps.md)).
- **Guild events** — cooperative objectives (see [Guilds.md](Guilds.md)).

## Dynamic Event System

Events are driven by world state and schedules. The system can spawn, escalate and resolve events
based on triggers (time, player activity, boss cycles), keeping the world reactive without manual
intervention. This complements dynamic quests.

## Rewards & Pacing

- Events grant time-limited, worthwhile rewards that respect the economy.
- Cadence is tuned so there is usually something happening without overwhelming players.
- Participation is accessible to solo players while scaling up for guilds.
