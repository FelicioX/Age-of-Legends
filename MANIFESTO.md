# The Age of Legends Manifesto

We are building a world, not shipping a feature list.

## What We Believe

**Play is the point.** Every system exists to create a moment worth remembering — a hard-won boss,
a kingdom that finally clicks, a trade that turns the market. If a mechanic does not serve the
player's experience, it does not belong.

**Worlds should persist.** A living world keeps turning whether or not anyone is watching.
Resources regrow, rivals scheme, events unfold. Progress earned is progress kept.

**Fairness is non-negotiable.** Power is earned through skill, planning and time — never bought. A
new player and a veteran meet on honest ground.

**Engineering is craft.** Clean architecture, determinism and tests are not overhead; they are what
let a small idea grow into a world that lasts for years without collapsing under its own weight.

**Originality is a discipline.** We study how great worlds are built and how great games feel, then
we build our own. Every name, place, hero and line is ours.

**The world belongs to its players.** Guilds, economies and shared events turn individual play into
collective story. We build the stage; players write the legends.

## How We Work

- Design before code. Test before merge. Document before we forget.
- Measure before we optimize. Decide in the open and record why.
- Add nothing that we cannot maintain for the long haul.

## Our Promise

To build a world that respects your time, rewards your mastery, remembers your deeds, and stays
worth returning to — for years.

— Felicio Xavier, and everyone who joins the build.
