# Retention — Analysis & Adaptation

## Pattern

Long-term genre retention comes from **rhythm and freshness**: daily/weekly goals, time-gated
production, social commitments and seasonal content combine so players have both a reason to check
in today and a reason to keep playing next month.

## Age of Legends Interpretation

See [../../docs/gdd/LiveOps.md](../../docs/gdd/LiveOps.md) and
[../../docs/gdd/GameLoop.md](../../docs/gdd/GameLoop.md):

- **Daily/weekly goals** with escalating rewards.
- **Time-gated production/construction** that rewards returning.
- **Social commitments** — guild goals and scheduled cooperative events.
- **Seasons** — rotating themed content and cosmetic chases.

## Guardrails (Ethical Retention)

- Respect the player's time; automate busywork.
- No dark patterns, no pay-to-win, transparent odds and pricing.
- Meaningful sessions at any length.

## Why It Is Original

The specific goals, cadences, seasons and rewards are designed for Age of Legends; only the
rhythm/freshness retention principle is a genre convention.
