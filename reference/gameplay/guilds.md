# Guilds & Social — Analysis & Adaptation

## Pattern

Social systems drive long-term retention through **belonging and interdependence**: shared goals,
roles, cooperative content and competition give players reasons to return that are external to their
own progress.

## Age of Legends Interpretation

See [../../docs/gdd/Guilds.md](../../docs/gdd/Guilds.md):

- **Roles and permissions** to keep large guilds manageable.
- **Shared progression** (guild level, perks, hall) funded by contributions.
- **Cooperative content** — guild events and contribution-based world bosses.
- **Competition** — territory control and guild rankings.

## Guardrails

- Casual members contribute meaningfully; dedicated cores drive competitive play.
- Contribution and rewards are transparent and audited to avoid abuse.

## Why It Is Original

Guild features, perks, territory rules and rewards are Age of Legends' own; only the principle of
interdependence-driven retention is a genre convention.
