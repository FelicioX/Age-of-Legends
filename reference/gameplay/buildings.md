# Buildings & Base Evolution — Analysis & Adaptation

## Pattern

An upgradable base creates **ownership and a long investment horizon**. A central building gates
overall progression; time-gated upgrades pace the game and reward returning players.

## Age of Legends Interpretation

See [../../docs/gdd/Buildings.md](../../docs/gdd/Buildings.md):

- **Town hall** gates the tier of all other buildings and features.
- **Production buildings** (farm, mine, quarry, sawmill) feed the five resources.
- **Function buildings** (barracks, laboratory, workshop, market, warehouse) unlock systems.
- **Adjacency bonuses** reward thoughtful layout without punishing experimentation.

## Guardrails

- Offline production up to storage capacity keeps the base idle-friendly.
- Protected resource portions keep raids fair (see [../../docs/gdd/PvP.md](../../docs/gdd/PvP.md)).
- Acceleration is convenience, never power.

## Why It Is Original

The building set, costs, curves, layout rules and art are Age of Legends' own; only the
central-gate/time-gated-upgrade structure is a genre convention.
