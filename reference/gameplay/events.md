# Events — Analysis & Adaptation

## Pattern

Events keep a persistent world **fresh and urgent**. A steady cadence of time- and trigger-based
occurrences gives players new goals and reasons to gather, without demanding constant new systems.

## Age of Legends Interpretation

See [../../docs/gdd/Events.md](../../docs/gdd/Events.md):

- **World events** tied to regions, weather and the day/night cycle.
- **World bosses** on schedules/triggers with contribution-based rewards.
- **Seasonal events** delivering themed goals and exclusive cosmetics.
- A **dynamic event system** that spawns, escalates and resolves events from world state.

## Guardrails

- Rewards are time-limited yet respect the economy.
- Cadence is tuned so something is usually happening without overwhelming players.
- Participation is accessible solo and scales up for guilds.

## Why It Is Original

Event types, triggers, rewards and themes are Age of Legends' own; only the "living-world cadence"
principle is a genre convention.
