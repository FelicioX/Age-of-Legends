# Progression — Analysis & Adaptation

## Pattern

The genre sustains engagement with **continuous, multi-track progression**: several parallel
systems advance at different rates so a player always has something to improve. Short-term wins are
frequent; long-term milestones are memorable.

## Age of Legends Interpretation

Parallel tracks with distinct cadences (see [../../docs/gdd/000_GDD.md](../../docs/gdd/000_GDD.md)):

- **Kingdom level** (slow, gating) — unlocks buildings, capacities and features.
- **Heroes** (medium) — levels, star ranks, skills, equipment.
- **Research** (slow, persistent) — durable account-wide bonuses.
- **Professions** (medium) — gathering and crafting mastery.
- **Season/account** (rotating) — cosmetic and prestige tracks.

## Pacing Guardrails

- Early game is generous and guided (front-load fun).
- Later tiers emphasize specialization and trade over raw grind.
- Power growth is bounded so skill and composition stay decisive.
- Catch-up mechanics keep newer players within reach of the active population.

## Why It Is Original

The tracks, curves, unlock orders, names and numbers are designed specifically for Age of Legends;
only the principle of parallel, continuous progression is drawn from the genre.
